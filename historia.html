In The Beginning, Before Time Began…

High above the dark void of space, when no worlds existed; from nigh the Holy Realm of the Heavens there shone a light. And as the light grew brighter, there came a voice. So powerful and sacred was that voice that if mortal man had been present, they would not have lived. And in the voice being spoken there was brought forth a single phrase: “ Come Doth Forth, Ye First World, and Shroud Thyself in Light.” After these words had been spoken by the Great Deity, there arose a great light, covering the void of darkness, and the two realms were defined, and the first world existed then.

Now the features of the world were created, and the beasts of the earth were brought forth, and all things of the earth, and of the two realms, and of everything in existence was created. Then… the voice spoke again and said “May the Peoples of the First World be Placed on it to Dwell. And the inhabitants of the world were brought forth to the earth, and dwelt on it. And He appointed the supreme forces, to watch over the peoples of the Earth, and instilled in the peoples one sovereign command: that they should not seek to know the deep power that He possessed.

Now that the world was created, and the Deity admired Their Creation of The First World, Time began, and everything was as it was intended. Alas, high in the Heavens, there was a stirring between the forces, and the forces of evil broke away from the forces of good, and defiled the gods by deceiving them into joining them in their scheme, because they sought to be like the Great Deity Himself. And many of the gods were taken to the side of evil.

Now the gods of evil descended to the earth and spoke among the peoples, telling them of the power they could have if they joined them, that they would be greater than the Great Deity even. And the people fell, and rebelled against their Creator, but He was aware of all this, and commanded the forces of good to summon a new people from the peoples already on the earth: the one… the four… the six… and these were indeed the chosen ones, known as the righteous ancients of good, who waged war with the evil ones; the ancients of evil. Now when the forces of good prevailed over the evil ones, by the power of the ancients, they instilled a law for the peoples, and then spread out to the lands were they were from.

Now the people could not keep the law, for it was sacred, and by the deep powers, were subject to death, and even the ancients themselves could not keep it entirely. But since the Great Deity loved his creation, He embodied his only offspring and sent him to the world, to be smitten by the evil ones, for only he was righteous enough to keep the sacred law. But after the evil ones had put Him to death, he arose again, and ascended into the heavens, so that all of the peoples may live free, so long as they trusted Him.

Now, these things took place in the days of the Ancients, and the Great Deity appointed His chosen ones to provide justice to the lands, and to watch over the peoples of the World. Now all of these things took place in a span of one hundred years. First Era of Time.

After all of these things had come to pass, there began the long span of peace for the world, which lasted only to the end of the said age of 10,000. Only few calamities took place during this time, for the Great Deity was limiting the evil ones’ power.

One thing that did occur during the said age was the nativity of the evil ancient Lord Black. During the beginning of the span of peace, while the ancients of good were unaware, a certain god called Moldorm descended to the earth from the place of the evil ones in the heavens. After he had come, Moldorm found a certain descendant of the evil ancients called Hysheria, who was a mortal. And together, they bore the Lord of Darkness, suppressor of good, who was Lord Black.

Now it came to pass that Lord Black grew strong in power over the years, and was gifted in powers and in magic and partial immorality, because of his decent. So he grew jealous of his father’s greater powers, and killed Moldorm. When Moldorm had been slain by his offspring, he returned to the heavens, and his anger kindled against Lord Black for destroying his earthly and embodied spirit, so he smote him and limited his power under subjection of the forces of evil. So Lord Black was limited at the time, but the forces did not allow Moldorm to kill Lord Black, for they perceived that he could be used on earth.

Now this is the end of the first age, which included the one hundred first years, and the span of peace. And all of these things of the first era were 10,000 years.
