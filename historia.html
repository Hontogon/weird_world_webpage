In The Beginning, Before Time Began…

High above the dark void of space, when no worlds existed; from nigh the Holy Realm of the Heavens there shone a light. And as the light grew brighter, there came a voice. So powerful and sacred was that voice that if mortal man had been present, they would not have lived. And in the voice being spoken there was brought forth a single phrase: “ Come Doth Forth, Ye First World, and Shroud Thyself in Light.” After these words had been spoken by the Great Deity, there arose a great light, covering the void of darkness, and the two realms were defined, and the first world existed then. Now the features of the world were created, and the beasts of the earth were brought forth, and all things of the earth, and of the two realms, and of everything in existence was created. Then… the voice spoke again and said “May the Peoples of the First World be Placed on it to Dwell. And the inhabitants of the world were brought forth to the earth, and dwelt on it. And He appointed the supreme forces, to watch over the peoples of the Earth, and instilled in the peoples one sovereign command: that they should not seek to know the deep power that He possessed. Now that the world was created, and the Deity admired Their Creation of The First World, Time began, and everything was as it was intended.
